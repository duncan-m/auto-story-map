  • Shipped as Released is a switch in the toolbar after the filters, off by default. Turning it on redraws the board at once. The URL keeps it, so a link or reload shows the same board.
  • One Released lane comes first and holds every shipped story, under its own activity, whatever release it shipped in. Its title says which releases it draws from.
  • Unshipped stories stay in their release lanes. A release with nothing left to show drops out of view, here 0.2, 0.4, 0.7.3, 0.7.5 and 0.7.6.
